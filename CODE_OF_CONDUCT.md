# Code of conduct

PyYAeHMOP welcomes discussion about extended Hückel methods, reproducibility, parallel computation, electronic structure and open scientific software.

We expect contributors to communicate respectfully, share reproducible technical evidence, and critique scientific claims rather than individuals. Harassment, discriminatory behavior, threats, or deliberate disclosure of another contributor's private information are not acceptable.

Scientific disagreements are normal. Explain assumptions, convergence controls and the scope of a model when challenging another result, and give credit to source authors.

For ordinary community concerns, contact the project maintainers through GitHub issues or discussions. For sensitive behavior concerns, contact the maintainers privately via their GitHub profiles instead of posting identifying information publicly.

Maintainers may moderate, hide or remove posts that violate these expectations and may restrict participation where necessary.
